# Laptop speakers

Laptop drivers have unknown excursion, protection, crossover, and thermal limits unless the manufacturer documents them.

- Default sweep: 120 Hz–20 kHz, -30 dBFS, 75 dB SPL guard.
- Do not infer usable bass extension from a protection-limited response or boost below the measured roll-off.
- Measure left, right, and combined output when routing permits. Screen angle and reflective surfaces change the result, so record geometry.
- Prefer subtractive EQ above the low-frequency protection region. Keep boost disabled until repeat measurements and headroom evidence justify it.
- Calibration applies only to the measured output device and physical configuration.
