# General and powered speakers

- Record actual model, published F3 or bandwidth, sensitivity, impedance where relevant, listening distance, coordinates, and source provenance. Leave unknown fields unknown.
- Use the measured in-room roll-off and output headroom to bound crossover and EQ decisions.
- For stereo systems, evaluate each speaker and the combined pair. Reflections or asymmetric placement may dominate differences.
- For subwoofer integration, compare main, sub, and main+sub traces with a shared acoustic timing reference.
- Prefer moving the speaker, subwoofer, or listener when a persistent spatial null is the dominant defect.
