# Car audio

- Immobilize and safely ventilate the vehicle; do not measure while driving. Keep battery voltage stable without creating exhaust risk.
- Inventory every amplified channel, crossover, polarity, delay, gain, factory processing path, and seat position.
- Measure drivers individually before combined traces. Protect tweeters with an appropriate high-pass before broadband sweeps.
- Use acoustic timing and crossover-region phase/summation, not impulse-peak alignment alone.
- Measure at the primary seat and nearby head positions. A single-seat correction may worsen the passenger seat.
- Address rattles, clipping, polarity, gain structure, and placement before EQ. Do not boost cabin nulls.
- Preserve the DSP project and all amplifier settings before applying changes; re-measure every affected crossover pair afterward.
